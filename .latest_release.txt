# 0.6.13

Fixed
- A download no longer sticks at 99% when its category folder doesn't
  exist yet. The transfer's folder is created with any missing parents,
  every failed file or folder logs why, and a failed download is retried
  after 1, 2, 4 ... up to every 30 minutes while it's on put.io (#51)

New
- Removing a download in the *arr ("Remove from Download Client") also
  deletes its local copy in download_directory, as Transmission does,
  unless keep_downloads is on. Downloads of a removed transfer stop (#52)
